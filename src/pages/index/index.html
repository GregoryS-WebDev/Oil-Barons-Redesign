Beaumont Oil Barons Announce Coaching Staff for Inaugural 2026 Season

Dec 18, 2025 - Beaumont's inaugural coaching staff brings experience, leadership, and local ties to the 2026 Season.

The Beaumont Oil Barons of the Mid America League are proud to announce the hiring of Mason Ross as the franchise's first Manager and Jim Gilligan, one of the most accomplished and respected coaches in Texas baseball history, as the team's Pitching Coach.

The pair will lead the Oil Barons into their inaugural season at Vincent-Beck Stadium on the campus of Lamar University. The hiring of Ross and Gilligan marks a major milestone as the franchise gears up for Opening Day on May 2026.

Manager Ross is an accomplished baseball coach with five years of progressive experience developing athletes and cultivating championship-caliber performance. Known for his ability to mentor players both technically and mentally, Ross has built a reputation for elevating individual skill sets while fostering an enduring passion for the game. His coaching expertise spans form and technique instruction, endurance training, and the creation of practice environments that promote discipline, growth, and competitive excellence.

A former collegiate player at USU Eastern, he brings firsthand knowledge of the athlete experience and a clear understanding of how to nurture talent at every stage of development. Since joining the Ogden Raptors in 2021, Ross has played a pivotal role in shaping a winning culture—highlighted by the 2023 Pioneer League Championship and three consecutive playoff appearances.

His responsibilities have included catcher development, hitting approach instruction, bullpen and bench coaching roles, first-base coaching duties, and assisting with lineup and game management. Ross is also skilled in player evaluation, and data tracking. Known for his professionalism and relationship-building with coaching staffs across the Pioneer League, he continues to bring leadership, energy, and a team-first mindset to every role he serves.

“Mason is exactly the type of leader we want representing Beaumont,” said Mike Marek, Oil Barons General Manager. “He's young, driven, innovative, and he understands how to develop players while also building a brand of baseball that fans will rally around. He brings professionalism and excitement to everything he touches.”

A Southeast Texas icon, longtime Lamar University Head Coach Jim Gilligan joins the Oil Barons as Pitching Coach, bringing a legendary résumé that includes more than 1,300 collegiate victories, multiple NCAA Tournament appearances, and recognition as one of the most respected baseball minds in the country.

Known for producing polished pitching staffs and helping launch the careers of numerous professional players, Gilligan adds championship-level experience to the coaching room.

“Having Coach Gilligan on our staff is an incredible honor,” Marek said. “He's a teacher, a mentor, and a fierce competitor. Our pitchers are going to learn from one of the best to ever do it—not just in Texas, but anywhere.”

With Ross's leadership and Gilligan's deep knowledge of pitching and player development, the Oil Barons aim to establish a winning culture from Day One. Both coaches emphasized their commitment to developing players, building a disciplined and exciting style of play, and creating a home-field environment that represents Beaumont's energy, toughness, and pride.

“The Oil Barons aren't just bringing baseball back to Beaumont—we're building something the entire region can be proud of,” said Marek. “With Mason and Jim leading the way, we know we're in great hands.”

Launched in 2024, the Mid America League has quickly become one of the premier summer baseball leagues in America. The league features top collegiate players alongside post-college players pursuing professional baseball careers. These athletes include players who were undrafted, unsigned free agents, or former professionals working to return to affiliated baseball. The Oil Barons will play from mid-May to mid-August.

The Mid-America League currently consists of franchises in:

  • Texarkana, TX
  • White Oak/Longview, TX
  • Nacogdoches, TX
  • Bryan, TX
  • Joplin, MO
  • Fort Scott, KS
  • Meridian, MS

The Mid America League is committed to growing and strengthening baseball in the region.

Oil Barons Seek Families to Host Players For '26 Season

Nov 22, 2025 - We're seeking families to host our baseball players for the summer!

We're seeking families to host our baseball players for the summer! Our players, typically college-age young men, will arrive in late May and stay through early August. During that time, they'll be busy with workouts, practices, and traveling for games, leaving them little time to focus on anything else.

That's where you come in.

As a host family, you primarily provide a place to stay. You'll be helping these young athletes stay focused and motivated to pursue their dreams of playing professional baseball.

Hosting a player is an opportunity to make a real, lasting impact on their journey, offering them the stability and support they need to succeed.

With a season consisting of 32 home games and 32 away games, players are constantly on the move. While they're busy with their commitments to the team, they'll appreciate a safe, comfortable home to which they can return.

Your support provides them with the foundation to stay focused on their training and games, without the added stress of worrying about where they'll stay.

Expectations for a Host Family include the following accommodations from mid-May through the first week of August:

  • Private bedroom with access to a bathroom.
  • Use of laundry room for everyday clothes (Uniforms are managed by the organization).
  • Breakfast, lunch, and snacks should be available for your player(s). An open fridge policy works best so that your player feels at home fixing his meals. Additionally, the team will provide post-game meals. These players are high-level athletes and tend to be conscious of what they eat. Having a variety of proteins and healthy carbs available for them is helpful.
  • Access to your home. Your player(s) will need to have access to come and go from your home if you are not there. Whether it be a key, garage door opener, or keycode. The players will have an active schedule this summer and at times are not home until the early hours of the morning.
  • Host families are welcome to invite the player to be a part of their family meals, TV time or other activities. In most cases, as your relationship with your player grows, you will find him to be “one of the family.” These guys will keep odd hours with late-night games and a crazy travel schedule, so they may not always eat breakfast! Keep in mind that half of these games will be on the road where the players are provided lodging and meals where the games are being played.
  • Respect your player and their need for rest. Be prepared for your player(s) to sleep in late, as their schedule is rigorous. The players will have over sixty games during the summer, with only one scheduled day off each week.
  • Because the players are responsible for driving to and from the field, We request that you are located within 25 minutes of the ballpark (Vincent-Beck Stadium, Lamar University).

Communication is key: Once the player arrives, we will address with them the expectations of their host families and we would encourage you to do the same. Exchange cell numbers with your player(s) so they can stay in communication with you. Lastly, these are all adults – some that have more than likely stayed with host families at some point – but may need some old-fashioned parental advice when they are far from home.

What you can expect as a Beaumont Oil Barons Host Family:

  • Respect: Players are expected to follow your specific house rules. Upon arrival, give your player(s) an indication of any house-specific rules by which your family abides. They are a guest in your home and will be expected to act as such. If any issues arise, we will solve the problem, move the player, or send the player home.
  • Underage Drinking: This is not allowed and is strictly prohibited for all the players.
  • Guests: Players will not be allowed to have guests in your home without your permission.
  • Summer Plans: Host families are encouraged to keep their schedules, including vacations. If you are not comfortable leaving your player at your home while you are away, arrangements can be made.
  • Transportation: Your player will have their vehicle, and you will not be responsible for providing transportation. (Unless prior arrangements have been made)
  • Support from Team Management: If any issues arise, the management team will work with you to provide a solution.

Why Host an Oil Baron?

  • Be Part of the Team – Host families become an extension of the Oil Baron family and play a vital role in our players' success.
  • Stronger Community Connections – Meet other host families, team staff, and fans while being part of something special.
  • Another Role Model – If you have children, bringing a college athlete into your home gives your kids a chance to bond with a positive role model who can inspire hard work, teamwork, and big dreams.
  • Behind-the-Scenes Experience – Get an inside look at the daily life of a college baseball player. Some of the next MLB stars will be playing in collegiate summer leagues this summer—who knows, maybe a future big leaguer will be staying in your home.
  • Lifelong Bonds – Many host families build lasting friendships with their players that go well beyond the summer. Players often become part of the family, forming connections with kids and serving as role models both on and off the field.
  • Recognition & Appreciation – We celebrate our host families with special events, on-field recognition, and social media shoutouts.
  • Impact a Player's Future – Your support helps these athletes chase their dreams while representing our community.
  • A Fun & Memorable Summer – Experience the excitement of Oil Baron's baseball in a whole new way.

PERKS:

  • Four (4) Reserved Season Tickets
  • Invitations to Special Events
  • Host family recognition during home games (dates TBD)
  • Discounts on Oil Barons merchandise
  • Discounts on Concession
  • Lifelong relationship with your baseball player!

Interested in Hosting? If you'd like to make a difference in a young athlete's life while enjoying an unforgettable summer of baseball, we would love to hear from you! Email Mike Marek at mike@oilbaronsbaseball.com or call (409) 919-4228. Thank you for being part of the Oil Baron's family!

Beaumont Baseball Franchise Unveils Team Name, Logo

Nov 22, 2025 - The Beamont-based Mid-America League expansion franchise will be known as the Oil Barons.

The Beaumont Baseball Franchise of the Mid-America League proudly announced today that the team will officially be known as the Beaumont Oil Barons.

Alongside the name, the organization revealed its new primary and secondary logos — a striking blend of modern design and classic Texas grit — marking a major milestone in the team's journey toward its inaugural season in May 2026.

The Oil Barons name pays tribute to Beaumont's historic roots as the birthplace of the Texas oil boom. The discovery of Spindletop in 1901 ignited an era of industry, innovation, and bold ambition — qualities the team aims to embody both on and off the field.

“The Oil Baron represent everything that makes Beaumont great — strength, pride, and a pioneering spirit that still fuels this region today,” said Mike Marek, General Manager of the Beaumont Baseball Franchise. “This name connects our community's past to its future, and we can't wait to see our players wear it across their chests next summer.”

The primary Beaumont Oil Barons logo strikes the perfect balance between power and prestige. The centerpiece features a commanding Great Dane with bat on shoulder dressed in a tuxedo set against the iconic state of Texas. The dignified canine embodies the “Oil Barons” spirit — strong, intelligent, and refined — while symbolizing leadership and confidence both on and off the field.

The team's secondary logo features a towering metallic gold oil derrick and a stylized “Beaumont Oil Barons” wordmark accented in gold, black and white, symbolizing the city's enduring connection to energy and progress.

The Beaumont Oil Barons will play its home games at Vincent-Beck Stadium on the campus of Lamar University, where fans can expect a family-friendly experience infused with local fla-vor, community pride, and big-league entertainment.

“This is more than a baseball team — it's a reflection of Beaumont's story,” said Marek. “The Oil Barons are here to celebrate our heritage, entertain our fans, and build something lasting for this community.”

Continue to explore the official team website for updates, news, season ticket information, and team merchandise featuring the new logo and color scheme.
